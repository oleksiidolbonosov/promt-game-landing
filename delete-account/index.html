Delete your Promptlyss account

This page explains how to request deletion of a Promptlyss guest account and associated game data.

Delete from the game

  1. Open Promptlyss and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account, then confirm the request.
  3. Keep the game online until it confirms completion. If the request fails, retry when connected or contact support below.

Deletion removes the guest profile and linked leaderboard and challenge records from the game service, requests deletion of the associated cloud backup, clears local game progress, and cancels local notifications scheduled by Promptlyss. Deletion is permanent and your local progress cannot be restored from that account.

If you cannot open the game

Email doogamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject Promptlyss account deletion request. Tell us the display name used in the game and whether you used Android or iOS. Do not send passwords, guest tokens, recovery codes, or payment-card details. We may contact you if we need limited information to identify the guest account and verify the request.

We will process the request within the period required by applicable law and let you know if we cannot locate or verify the account. Email requests are for account deletion; they do not cancel purchases or subscriptions through Apple or Google.

Data not automatically removed by account deletion

The in-game deletion flow does not automatically send deletion requests to Unity Analytics, advertising partners, or app stores for records those providers hold separately from the Promptlyss guest account. Include a request for those records in your email if you want us to help route or handle it. Some records may be retained by a provider or by us where required or permitted by law.

Read the Privacy Policy deletion section or contact Promptlyss support for more information.
