Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Who We Are / Data Controller

Promptlyss is operated by Individual Entrepreneur Oleksii Dolbonosov (ФОП Долбоносов Олексій Олексійович), Ukraine, operating under the DOO Games Studio brand. The individual entrepreneur is the operator and data controller for Promptlyss. DOO Games Studio is a brand/trading name, not a separate company. In this Policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the individual entrepreneur operating Promptlyss under that brand.

2. Scope

This Policy covers information processed when you use the Promptlyss mobile game and its guest profiles, gameplay, progress, leaderboards, challenges, cloud-backup feature, analytics, advertising integrations, purchases and local notifications. Apple App Store and Google Play process information under their own terms and privacy notices when you use their services.

3. Information You Provide

You may choose a display name for your guest profile and leaderboard activity. When you use challenge features, the game processes challenge content and the choices and attempts associated with it. If you email us, we receive the message and any information you choose to include.

4. Guest Account and Display Name

Promptlyss can create a guest profile for online features. The profile uses a pseudonymous player identifier and an authentication credential. A display name may be submitted to the online service and shown with leaderboard activity. A pseudonymous identifier is not anonymous information: it can be associated with the related game profile and activity.

5. Gameplay and Progress Data

Depending on the features you use, gameplay and progress information can include scores, answers and attempts, experience and level progress, coins, unlocked packs, stars, streaks, Daily or Endless mode progress, rewards and related gameplay events. Some profile and progression data is stored locally on your device. Online features may send relevant information to game services.

6. Leaderboards and Challenges

For leaderboards, Promptlyss submits and retrieves scores and associated gameplay context. Your chosen display name may appear with leaderboard activity. Challenge features process challenge content, player-linked challenge records, attempts, answers and results. Challenge links may provide access to a challenge to people who receive the link; share them accordingly.

7. Cloud Backup

If you use cloud backup, Promptlyss sends a compressed profile and progress snapshot to the cloud-backup service configured for that game version. The snapshot includes a pseudonymous backup identifier and may include the profile and progression information described above. The identifier is derived from device information and is not anonymous. Cloud backup depends on an internet connection and may be unavailable or fail to preserve every version of your progress. The storage provider and its technical retention controls may depend on the service configuration.

8. Analytics

Promptlyss includes Unity Services Analytics integration and can send gameplay and user-interface events, such as feature use, progress events, and whether an account-deletion attempt succeeds or fails. Analytics may also process technical identifiers and diagnostics under Unity's service configuration. We do not describe these records as anonymous. Account deletion in Promptlyss does not currently send a deletion request to Unity Analytics; contact us if you want to request deletion of analytics data associated with your device or installation.

9. Advertising

Promptlyss includes Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay advertising integrations and may display ads depending on the version, platform, region, and release configuration. LevelPlay may involve third-party ad networks enabled for that release. When an advertising service is enabled, it may process device and advertising identifiers, IP/network information, ad impressions, and interactions for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or personalization as permitted by applicable settings and law. Advertising partners apply their own privacy notices and retention rules; the active network list depends on the release configuration.

10. Purchases

Promptlyss uses Unity In-App Purchasing with Apple App Store or Google Play billing, as applicable to your device and the available product. Purchase handling can receive product information, transaction identifiers, receipts and purchase status to process an entitlement. Payment credentials are handled through the applicable store's purchase flow.

11. Device and Technical Information

The cloud-backup snapshot includes a pseudonymous hash derived from a device identifier. Online services and advertising or analytics SDKs may also receive technical information such as app version, device or operating-system information, IP/network information, and advertising identifiers, depending on the feature and release configuration. We do not treat pseudonymous identifiers as anonymous.

12. Notifications

Promptlyss supports local notifications scheduled on your device. Your device's operating-system settings control notification permission and delivery.

13. Purposes of Processing

We process information to create and maintain guest profiles; provide gameplay, progress, leaderboard, challenge and backup features; record gameplay and interface events through analytics; operate advertising integrations; process purchases and entitlements; schedule local notifications; and protect game services from cheating and abuse.

14. Legal Bases Where Applicable

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process account and online-feature data as needed to provide the feature you use; process security and abuse-prevention data for our legitimate interests in keeping the game and its services reliable; process purchase records to provide entitlements and meet legal obligations; and rely on consent where consent is required for analytics or advertising. The applicable basis depends on the feature, release configuration, and your location. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. Contact us if you cannot find a privacy choice for a feature available in your version.

15. Service Providers / Third Parties

Online guest profiles, leaderboards, and challenges use Supabase services. Cloud backup is sent to the cloud endpoint configured for the game version. Unity Services Analytics processes analytics events; Unity Ads may serve ads directly; and Unity LevelPlay may mediate advertising to configured network partners. Unity In-App Purchasing and Apple App Store or Google Play handle in-app purchase flows, as applicable. The specific ad networks and cloud storage provider can vary by release configuration; the operating system, app store, and each enabled provider may process information under their own privacy notices.

16. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will use a legally recognized transfer mechanism or another permitted safeguard. Contact us for information about transfers relevant to your request.

17. Data Retention

Some profile and progress data remains on your device until you delete your account, clear the app's data, or uninstall the game, subject to the device platform's behavior. Online profile, leaderboard, challenge, and backup data is kept while needed to provide the feature, until it is deleted through the account-deletion flow, or for a longer period where required for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Analytics, advertising, app-store, and cloud providers may retain records under their own settings and policies. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider; contact us for details about a specific request.

18. Data Security

We use service providers and technical measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not share your recovery code or account credentials with other people.

19. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to receive information in a portable form, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Depending on the law that applies where you live, these rights may include access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, a copy in a portable format, withdrawal of consent, and lodging a complaint with your data-protection authority. To exercise a right, use the account deletion request page or contact us. We may need to verify your request before acting.

20. Data Deletion / Privacy Requests

To delete your Promptlyss guest account in the game, open Settings → Delete account and confirm. The request needs an internet connection. When deletion completes, Promptlyss deletes the guest profile and linked leaderboard and challenge records from its game service, requests deletion of the linked cloud backup, clears the local game profile and progress, and cancels local notifications scheduled by the game. The game reports an error if it cannot confirm deletion of the cloud backup or game-service account; retry while online or contact us using the account deletion request page.

This in-game process does not automatically delete analytics, advertising, app-store, or other provider records held separately from the Promptlyss guest account. To request help with those records, or to exercise another privacy right, use the request page or email doogamesstudio@gmail.com. We may ask for limited information needed to locate and verify the request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

21. Children / Minimum Age

Promptlyss is not directed to children under the age of 13. You must be at least 13 years old to use Promptlyss, or older where required by applicable law. Promptlyss does not claim to verify a user's age through an in-game age gate.

We may update this Policy when the game or its data practices change. The updated version will show its revision date. Where applicable law requires notice or consent for a change, we will follow those requirements.

23. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator at doogamesstudio@gmail.com. DOO Games Studio is the operator's brand, not a separate legal entity.
